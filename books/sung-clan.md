## Chapter: Clan Heritage

### History

The Sung clan (성/成) traces its origins to the Changnyeong region (창녕지방) of Korea. Population (2015): 182,377. Chinese character: 成 · Korean: 성 · Romanized: Seong/Sung.

**Origins:** Originally from Chinese descent during the Song Dynasty. Progenitor: Seong In-bo (성인보), household head and Jungyun during Goryeo Dynasty. According to the "Joseon Clan Genealogy," In-bo's father was a descendant of Seong-jeo, a high-ranking Silla official. The government established surnames for civil service examinations.

**References:** http://www.sungs.kr · "Joseon Clan Genealogy" · "Abridged History of the Seong Clan"

---

### Clan Organization & Sects

**Thirteen Sects:** The surname Seong was founded by the only son of Jungyun-gong. He had two brothers: Gong Pil (판서공) and Han Pil (찬성공). Divided into:
- **Panseogong branch (판서공파)** — 10 branches formed in six generations
- **Chanseonggong branch (찬성공파)** — 3 branches formed in six generations

**The Jisa Sect (지사공파) — Our Branch:** Jisa (知事公, 彦臣), son of Jeonseogong (典書公) Jeo (貯), held position of Jirimjusa (知林州事), Bongsang Daeboo (奉常大夫).

Notable descendants: Munhyogong Sadal (renowned minister), Chongrangong Bu (loyal minister), Chungjeong-gong Huian (Chief State Councilor), Changseong-gun Huiong, Munjang-gong Sejang (incorruptible official).

**Primary regions:** Sangju (Gyeongbuk), Yangju (Gyeonggi), Yesan (Chungcheong), Yeonggwang/Gochang (Jeolla), Yeongdong (Chungbuk).

---

### Lineage & Naming

**Generation Ranking (항렬/行列):** Rank system based on five elements (五行) or six gaps (六甲順). Descendants use same character in alternating positions to indicate generation gap. Different branches had different ranking systems (Daedongbo, Hoegokpa, Noha).

**Unified Ranking (33rd Generation Onward):** In 1974, representatives of all Seong clans created a unified genealogy table. Starting from the 33rd generation, all members name according to this table.
